Xenorra is a protocol research and development project. This site describes the November 2026 draft specification, not a deployed, audited or benchmarked network. Estimates are analytical and subject to validation. XNR planning figures: 72.9B launch supply, 165B maximum supply, and a proposed 17% foundation allocation. The issuance schedule, vesting and other tokenomic terms remain subject to confirmation. Nothing here constitutes a financial offer.
