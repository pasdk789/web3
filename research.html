RESEARCH INTEGRITY

What remains unproven.

Xenorra’s paper does not present a finished network, independent audits or experimentally demonstrated performance.

Not quantum-proofPost-quantum resistance depends on security assumptions and future cryptanalytic results.
Not benchmarkedThe draft explicitly reports no measured performance results.
Not a live mainnetThe roadmap describes work to be completed, not already-delivered functionality.
Not final tokenomicsXNR economics remain to be specified and validated.
