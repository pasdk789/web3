XENORRA FORUM PREVIEW

Open forum.html and use Start a discussion. Posts and replies are stored in browser localStorage. Draft text is saved in sessionStorage while editing. No login is required for this preview.

IMPORTANT: This is NOT a public multi-user forum. Other devices and browsers cannot see the posts. Before publishing as a live community, connect a server-side database, authentication, email verification, spam controls, moderation, backups and access rules. Do not collect passwords in localStorage.
