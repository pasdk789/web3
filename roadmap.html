04 / ROADMAP

Build in stages.
Prove each one.

Ten research-to-mainnet phases, each subject to explicit acceptance criteria. The draft does not commit to calendar launch dates.

PHASED EXECUTION

From research to implementation.

The following is a development sequence from the research specification, not a claim that milestones have been completed.

01

Research

Research dossier, architectural decisions and external technical review.

02

Formal specification

Normative rules, test vectors and model checking of consensus.

03

Cryptographic foundation

Vetted primitives, serialization, domain separation and differential fuzzing.

04

Single-node prototype

Deterministic state transitions, storage and transaction validation.

05

Consensus & networking

Validator voting, transport, synchronization and peer behavior.

06

Private testnet

Multi-node operation, fault injection and light-client validation.

07

Security review

Independent audits, remediation and adversarial testing.

08

Performance work

Reproducible benchmarking, optimization and resource profiling.

09

Public testnet

Public network trials and operational hardening.

10

Mainnet readiness

Release gates, operational procedures and final launch review.

Dates, resources and completion status depend on funding, team size, independent validation and acceptance gates. No mainnet date is specified.
